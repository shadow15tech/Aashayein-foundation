PraveenLata Sansthan Foundation

PraveenLata Sansthan Foundation is an award-winning non-profit organization based in Jaipur working with its operations across PAN India.

The primary focus is to alleviate the problems of the poor and their struggle to obtain a life of justice and dignity focusing on Child Welfare, Women Empowerment and Rural Development.

An in-depth understanding of issues in the area and consistent support from community members have led the organization to undertake and implement various need-based interventions on education, health and skill development at grass root level.

Bharti Singh Chauhan

Founder & Director

President Awardee Bharti Singh Chauhan known as the Pad Woman of Rajasthan is the Founder & Director of PraveenLata Sansthan Foundation. The organization works to empower girls and women across India.

Bharti has challenged social barriers and dedicated her life to women's health awareness, education and empowerment. Through multiple programs and initiatives she has impacted thousands of women and families.

Her vision is to create a society where every woman and child has access to opportunities, dignity and a better future.
